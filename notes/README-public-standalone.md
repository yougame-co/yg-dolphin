## Standalone

YG Dolphin plays Brawl and official Project+ v3.2 offline without the YouGame app or an account.
Bring your own Brawl USA revision 2 disc image; the supported USA revision 1 dump plays as
revision 2. See the [player guide](../docs/PLAY-STANDALONE.md) for downloads, Windows and Mac
startup, controllers and Project+ setup.

Builds containing the standalone changes also accept `--pack <folder>` for unsigned local
tree or flat packs. This mode is offline only: public queues, ratings and friends rooms are
disabled. The already published 0.3.58 archives do not include this new option. Online play
still needs YouGame's servers, and signed community packs still open through YouGame.

Source: [yougame-co/orca-netplay](https://github.com/yougame-co/orca-netplay).
